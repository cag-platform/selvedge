·Your record

The longer you use it, the more it knows — and the easier it is to leave.

It runs on your keys

Your Claude subscription, your OpenAI account, your usage. Selvedge doesn’t make money selling you model tokens, so it has no reason to want your conversations longer than they need to be.

Every model signs its answer

You always know who said what. Two answers to one question stay two answers.

Unknown means unknown

Missing information doesn’t become zero. Unverified doesn’t become healthy.

Your history can leave

Conversations, project context, intent and outcome records, run information — all of it exports, whenever you want it.

A record you can’t leave with isn’t really yours.
